CSP SHIP UPGRADE TEST CHECKLIST 1.3.0

Use a copied save for this test.

1. Start Caribbean Legend and confirm the main menu and saved game load.
2. Visit at least one English, French, Spanish, and Dutch shipyard.
3. Select "I want to upgrade and refit my ship."
4. Confirm the CSP layout opens and Escape returns to the shipyard.
5. Confirm all fourteen rows have readable names, material icons, and
   Available / Not suitable / Installed status.
6. Test Merchant, Universal, and Warship template buttons on matching ships.
7. Select one upgrade manually, then remove it.
8. Select one to three upgrades and confirm:
   - upgrade count is correct;
   - material totals update;
   - construction time is reasonable;
   - deposit does not multiply after repeated selection changes.
9. Place the order and confirm the deposit is deducted once.
10. Speak to a different shipyard; it must refuse to complete that order.
11. Return to the original shipyard with only part of the requested cargo.
12. Confirm delivered cargo is removed and the remaining amount is retained.
13. Deliver the rest and complete the refit.
14. Confirm time advances and the chosen upgrades show as Installed.
15. Save, reload, and confirm the upgraded statistics remain.
16. Confirm standard sale/repair, ship commission, and appearance menus still
    open and close normally.
17. Confirm Black Pearl, Cursed Black Pearl, and Queen Anne's Revenge remain
    commissionable and keep their names and portraits.
18. Confirm Cursed Black Pearl fog and sweep command still work.
19. Confirm no Flying Dutchman, Kraken, or submerge command appears.
20. Check compile.log, error.log, and system.log after the pass.
